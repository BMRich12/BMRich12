### Hi, I'm Brody 👋

I make iPhone games on my own. Right now that's **[StepHaven](https://stephaven.app)**,
a cozy town builder you play by walking: your steps from Apple Health become points
you spend on cottages, gardens, rivers and trains.

- Written in Swift with SwiftUI and SpriteKit
- Every tree, cottage and gnome is drawn in code, with no image files for the map
- Your step counts never leave your phone
